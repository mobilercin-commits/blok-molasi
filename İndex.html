Blok Molası - Privacy Policy

Developer: HobiHub Apps
Last updated: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HobiHub Apps handles information when you use Blok Molası ("the App"), a block puzzle game supported by advertising.

1. Accounts and Personal Information

The App does not require registration or a user account. You do not need to provide your name, email address, or phone number to play. The current version does not include online leaderboards or player profiles.

Advertising services integrated into the App process certain information as described below. Therefore, the App should not be understood as collecting no data.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores game information locally to support gameplay and allow you to continue a saved game. This includes:

  • Saved board layouts, available pieces, scores, and progress
  • High scores and gameplay statistics
  • Bonus availability and rewarded-ad usage counters
  • Language, sound, vibration, and tutorial preferences
  • Technical game identifiers and advertising frequency counters

HobiHub Apps does not operate a dedicated server to store these game saves. Depending on your device and backup settings, Android may include local app data in system backups or transfer it when you move to another device.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded advertisements. Rewarded advertisements allow you to obtain an in-game bonus after meeting the advertisement's reward conditions.

Google's advertising services may collect and share information including IP addresses, approximate location derived from IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. Processing depends on applicable settings, consent choices, and service configuration.

This information is used for advertising delivery and measurement, analytics, security, and fraud prevention. Ad-related information may be processed by Google and advertising service providers.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps and sites .

4. Advertising Preferences and Consent

The App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request privacy choices where applicable. Where required, you can revisit the privacy options provided within the App.

You can also manage your advertising identifier and available advertising preferences through Android or Google settings. Non-personalized or limited advertisements may still involve data processing for purposes such as delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.

5. Device Access

The App uses internet access for advertising and related services, checks network availability, and may use vibration for gameplay feedback.

The current version does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location permissions. Approximate location may nevertheless be inferred from an IP address by advertising services.

6. Support Communications

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, your message, and any information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your request and investigate reported issues. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.

7. Sharing and International Processing

HobiHub Apps does not sell your game saves or support correspondence. Advertising-related information is processed by third-party services as described in this policy. Our email service provider also processes support correspondence.

Service providers may process information in countries other than your country of residence under their privacy practices and applicable safeguards. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law.

8. Retention and Deletion

Local game data is retained until it is overwritten or removed. You can delete local data using Android's app storage settings or by uninstalling the App. This removes saved progress and preferences. System backups may remain subject to your backup provider's settings and may restore data after reinstallation.

Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request and meet applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request its deletion.

Google and other service providers retain information according to their own policies. Deleting the App does not automatically delete information already processed by those providers.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your personal information, or to object to certain processing and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

Contact us about information we hold. For information controlled by Google, use Google's privacy controls and request channels. You may also have the right to contact your local data protection authority.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children. It does not provide a registration system for collecting children's personal information.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to HobiHub Apps, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

11. Security

We seek to limit unnecessary data collection and protect information handled by the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Third-party services apply their own security measures.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the App's features or data practices change. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated date. Additional notice or consent will be provided where required.
